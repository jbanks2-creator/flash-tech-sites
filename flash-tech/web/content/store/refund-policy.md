## The short version

- **Download tier: 14 days, no questions asked, full refund.** If a $49 download is not worth keeping, we would rather give the money back than argue about it.
- **Configured tier ($99): refundable until the configuration is delivered.** The 30-minute call can happen and you can still change your mind; once your client list and projects are pre-loaded and handed over, the work is done and the sale is final.
- **Your data is not affected by a refund.** It is in your browser, not ours. Export it before you ask for a refund if you want to keep it.
- **A refund cancels the licence key.** The key stops working; you agree to stop using the app and delete your copies.
- **Ask us first.** A chargeback costs more than the refund and takes longer for you to get your money.

## 1. Download tier — $49, 14 days, no questions

You may request a full refund for **any reason or none** within **14 days of purchase**, provided you have not resold or redistributed the app or the key.

- **How:** reply to your receipt email, or email [JERRY: support email address] with the order number and the email address used at checkout. "I'd like a refund, order #1234" is enough — you do not have to explain why.
- **Timing:** we authorise the refund within **2 business days** of the request; the money lands back on your original payment method as fast as the merchant of record and your bank process it (typically 5–10 business days).
- **Who pays it:** the merchant of record is the legal seller and issues the credit. We authorise it — you do not have to chase them separately.
- **Partly used, still refunded.** Using the app for 13 days and then asking for a refund is allowed. The window is the rule, not the amount of use. This is the download tier only — the configured tier follows §2, where delivery is the line, not the clock.

**After 14 days** the sale is final as far as "changed my mind" goes. ## 2. Configured tier — $99, refundable until delivery

The configured tier is the download plus **your client list and projects pre-loaded and a 30-minute setup call**. The 14-day no-questions rule is written for a file you can simply stop using; on this tier the work lands on your own data at your instruction, so the line is **delivery**, not a stopwatch:

| Milestone | What has happened | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| **A — nothing delivered yet** | You have paid. The 30-minute call may or may not have happened. Your client list and projects are not yet loaded and handed over | **100% refund, $99.** Any time before we deliver, for any reason or none. |
| **B — configuration delivered** | Your client list and projects are pre-loaded and the app has been handed over to you | **No refund** — the work is done. Defects in the app are fixed free under the support policy. |
| **C — we fail to deliver** | We cannot or will not complete the configuration | **100% refund, $99.** |

- **What "delivered" means:** the app handed to you with your client list and projects pre-loaded — during the 30-minute call or by email afterwards. If you buy the tier and never schedule the call, nothing has been delivered and **A** still applies.
- **Support time is separate.** Paid support at **$145/hour** is work delivered, not part of the $99, and is not refunded once it has been done. A defect in the app is never charged in the first place (see the support policy).

## 4. Chargebacks

Please email us before contacting your bank. A chargeback freezes the payment while it is investigated and usually takes longer than a refund. We will not dispute a chargeback that we would have refunded anyway — but a chargeback filed instead of asking us means the licence is cancelled immediately and a repeat pattern can end our willingness to sell to that customer. `[ATTORNEY: any consumer-protection issue with discouraging chargebacks, and can we refuse future sales?]`
