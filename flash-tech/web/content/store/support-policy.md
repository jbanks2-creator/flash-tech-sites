## The short version

- **Email support is included for 12 months** from the day you buy. There is no monthly hour allowance and no contract to renew.
- **After those 12 months it is $145/hour**, quoted before we start, and you can always say no.
- **We reply by the next business day.** Reply, not fix. A one-line answer counts as support.
- **We fix our defects for free** — in the version you bought, for the life of that version, whether you report it in month one or year four.
- **We do not fix your computer, your browser, your data or your wish-list.** Those are either your side of the line or a paid job.
- **Email only — there is no phone support.** If you need to talk it through, ask in the email and we will book a call; a booked call is paid work, not an open phone line.

**Bugs are always free.** If the app does something the documentation says it should not do, that is our problem and it does not touch your included support. Fixing it may not be immediate, but it is not billable — and it stays free for the life of the version you bought, long after the 12 months.

## 5. Escalation — still email

There is **no phone support and no escalation number.** A phone line answered by one person would quietly become the service level this whole policy exists to avoid promising.

- If email has stalled, or something is urgent and business-critical, reply to your last support email with **"escalate"** in the subject and your order number in the body.
- An **S1** (see §3) is acknowledged the same business day and work starts immediately. Everything else is picked up by the next business day.
- It is for **existing customers with a live problem**, not a sales line.
- If you need to talk it through, say so in the email. We will book a call at a mutually agreed time — a booked call is paid work under §2, not an open phone line.
